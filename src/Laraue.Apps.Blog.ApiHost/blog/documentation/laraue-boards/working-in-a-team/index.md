When you're ready to bring in teammates, Laraue Boards organizations give everyone a shared workspace. This section covers the full setup from creating an org to fine-tuning who can do what.

## In this section

- [Creating an organization](/blog/documentation/laraue-boards/working-in-a-team/creating-organization) — Create the org, generate an invite link, and get your first teammate in.

- [Permissions management](/blog/documentation/laraue-boards/working-in-a-team/permissions) — Viewing your member roster, understanding roles, and the permission model: administrative tools, organization-wide access, and per-space access.

## How quickly can a team get started?

Creating an org and inviting a teammate takes under two minutes. The invite is a link — send it in Telegram. No email, no onboarding form, no waiting. A new member starts with no permissions at all, though, so plan to configure their access right after they join.
