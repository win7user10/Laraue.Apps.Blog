# Working in a team — collaboration guide

When you're ready to bring in teammates, Laraue Boards organizations give everyone a shared workspace. This section covers the full setup from creating an org to fine-tuning who can see and edit what.

## In this section

- [Creating an organization](/blog/documentation/laraue-boards/working-in-a-team/creating-organization) — Step-by-step: create the org, generate an invite link, and get your first teammate in.

- [Permissions management](/blog/documentation/laraue-boards/working-in-a-team/permissions) — The permission model explained. How organization, space, and epic level grants interact, and how to configure them per member.

- [Member management](/blog/documentation/laraue-boards/working-in-a-team/member-management) — Viewing the member roster, changing roles, and revoking access.

## How quickly can a team get started?

Creating an org and inviting a teammate takes under two minutes. The invite is a link — send it in Telegram. No email, no onboarding form, no waiting.
