An organization lets multiple people share boards and collaborate on the same issues. Setting one up takes under two minutes.

## Step 1 — Create the organization

From the workspace selection screen (available after login or via **Manage organizations** in the breadcrumb), tap **New organization**.

Fill in:
- **Name** — your company, team, or project name
- **Key** — a short abbreviation (2–4 characters) shown in the workspace switcher and used in board key prefixes
- **Color** — identifies your org in the breadcrumb and switcher

Tap **Create**. You are immediately set as the Owner.

## Step 2 — Enter the organization

Tap the organization tile on the workspace screen. You are now working inside the shared organization workspace. Any boards or spaces you create here will be visible to members you invite.

## Step 3 — Generate an invite link

Open the **user avatar** menu (top right corner) and tap **My organization**. On the Members tab, you will see an **Invite link** card.

Tap **Copy link** and send the link to your teammate in Telegram, by email, or however you prefer. When they open the link:

1. They are prompted to log in with Telegram if not already logged in
2. They see your organization name and a confirmation screen
3. They tap **Accept** and join as a Member

## Step 4 — Verify the member joined

Return to the Members tab. The new member will appear in the list with the **Member** role. You can change their role to **Admin** if needed.

## Managing the invite link

The invite link does not expire automatically. To invalidate it — for example if you shared it publicly by mistake — tap **Regenerate**. A new link is created and the old one stops working. Existing members are not affected.

## What new members see

New members immediately see all boards and spaces in the organization according to the default permission settings. If you want more granular control — for example, allowing a contractor to see only one specific space — configure permissions before sharing the invite link.

See [Permissions management](/blog/documentation/laraue-boards/working-in-a-team/permissions).
