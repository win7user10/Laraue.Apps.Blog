**Settings → Data movement** in the sidebar lets you move a space or a board out of the current organization. Moving something takes everything inside it along — a space brings all its boards and their issues; a board brings all its issues.

![The Data movement page, with separate Spaces and Boards sections](https://laraue.com/static/images/blog/docs/laraue-boards/data-movement.jpg)

## Moving spaces

Under **Spaces**, check the ones you want to move, or use the swap icon on a single row to move just that one. Tap **Move spaces**, choose the destination organization, and confirm.

![The Move space dialog, with an organization dropdown](https://laraue.com/static/images/blog/docs/laraue-boards/move-space.jpg)

## Moving boards

Under **Boards**, boards are grouped by the space they currently belong to. Check the ones you want to move, or use the swap icon on a single row. Tap **Move boards**, choose the destination organization and the space within it, and confirm.

![The Move board dialog, with organization and space dropdowns](https://laraue.com/static/images/blog/docs/laraue-boards/move-board.jpg)

## Who can do this

Moving spaces and boards requires the **Move spaces and boards** administrative permission. See [Permissions management](/blog/documentation/laraue-boards/working-in-a-team/permissions).
