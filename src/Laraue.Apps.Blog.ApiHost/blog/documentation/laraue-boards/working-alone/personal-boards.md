Laraue Boards works just as well for one person as it does for a team. Your personal organization is private by default — no invites, nothing to configure, and it already exists the moment you log in.

## Your personal organization

When you log in, you land on the organization selection screen and can choose **Personal** — your own private organization. Everything you create here — boards, spaces, issues — is visible only to you.

![The organization selection screen, listing Personal and a team organization, with a Create organization button](https://laraue.com/static/images/blog/docs/laraue-boards/login-organization.jpg)

You can always create a team organization later and move boards into it. Personal boards stay personal unless you explicitly move them.

## The typical solo workflow

1. **Messages arrive in Telegram** — a client sends a request, a colleague flags a bug, you receive a task in a group chat
2. **Forward to the bot** — forward the message to **@msgboard_bot**. It becomes an issue on your default board immediately, no setup required
3. **Move it when you're ready** — open the issue whenever you get to it and change its board or status, if you want it somewhere other than the default
4. **Work the board** — drag cards through columns as you progress through the work
5. **Done** — cards in your Done column are out of the way but still searchable

## Keeping boards small

A common mistake is creating one large board for everything. Instead, create separate boards for each distinct area of work:

- One board per client
- One board per project
- One board for personal life tasks separate from work

Use spaces to group related boards together once you have more than four or five.

## Working offline

Laraue Boards requires an internet connection to sync with Telegram and load your boards. There is no offline mode currently.
