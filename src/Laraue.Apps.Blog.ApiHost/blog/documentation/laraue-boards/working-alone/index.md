Laraue Boards works just as well for one person as it does for a team. You don't need to create an organization, invite anyone, or configure permissions. Start with a personal workspace and add a team later if you need one.

## In this section

- [Personal boards](/blog/documentation/laraue-boards/working-alone/personal-boards) — How your personal workspace works, typical solo workflows, and tips for keeping boards manageable.

- [The Backlog](/blog/documentation/laraue-boards/working-alone/backlog) — Your inbox for unassigned issues. How to triage, sort, search, and assign issues from the Backlog.

- [Capturing Telegram messages](/blog/documentation/laraue-boards/working-alone/telegram-messages) — How to turn any Telegram message — text, photo, or video — into an issue with one forward.

## The solo workflow in brief

Forward a message to the bot → it lands in your Backlog → assign it to a board → work it through columns → done. That's the entire loop.
