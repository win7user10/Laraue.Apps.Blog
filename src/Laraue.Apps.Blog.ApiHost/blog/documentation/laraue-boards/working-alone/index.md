Laraue Boards works just as well for one person as it does for a team. Your personal organization is set up for you automatically the moment you log in — nothing to create, invite, or configure first.

## In this section

- [Personal boards](/blog/documentation/laraue-boards/working-alone/personal-boards) — How your personal organization works, typical solo workflows, and tips for keeping boards manageable.

- [The Backlog](/blog/documentation/laraue-boards/working-alone/backlog) — The default board every space starts with. How to browse, search, and move issues off it.

- [Capturing Telegram messages](/blog/documentation/laraue-boards/working-alone/telegram-messages) — How to turn any Telegram message — text, photo, or video — into an issue with one forward.

## The solo workflow in brief

Forward a message to the bot → it becomes an issue on your default board automatically → move it to another board or status whenever you get to it. That's the entire loop — nothing has to happen before the issue is usable.
