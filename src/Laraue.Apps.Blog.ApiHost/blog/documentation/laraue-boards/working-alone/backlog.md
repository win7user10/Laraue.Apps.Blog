The Backlog is the board every space starts with. Every issue you capture from Telegram lands here automatically — there's nothing to set up and nothing you have to do before an issue counts as usable.

## What the Backlog shows

The Backlog is a plain list of the issues currently on it — the same way any other board shows its issues, just without columns. Each row shows the issue's key, its content, and who it's assigned to.

![The Backlog showing a list of issues](https://laraue.com/static/images/blog/docs/laraue-boards/backlog-view.jpg)

## Searching the Backlog

Tap the search icon in the Backlog header. As you type, the list filters in real time. Press Escape or tap the icon again to clear.

## Moving an issue off the Backlog

Each issue row has a swap icon on the right — tap it to reassign the issue to a different board directly from the list, without opening the issue's full detail view.

You can also open the issue itself and change its board or space from there, the same way as on any other board.
