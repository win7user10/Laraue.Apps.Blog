## In this section

- [Authorization](/blog/documentation/laraue-boards/getting-started/authorization) — How to log in using your Telegram account, both inside the Mini App and in the browser. No password, no registration.

- [Quick start](/blog/documentation/laraue-boards/getting-started/quick-start) — A walkthrough of your first few minutes in Laraue Boards: send a message to the bot, watch it become an issue, and move it through statuses.

## Before you begin

You need a Telegram account. That's it. There is no separate sign-up, no email to verify, and no credit card required.

The moment you log in, you already have a personal organization with a default space, a board, and a Backlog set up — there's nothing to configure before you can start. Send a message to the bot and it becomes an issue right away, no setup required first.

Open [@msgboard_bot](https://t.me/msgboard_bot) in Telegram to get started, or visit [boards.laraue.com](https://boards.laraue.com) in your browser.
