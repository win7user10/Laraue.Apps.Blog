This section covers everything you need to go from zero to a working board with your first task on it.

## In this section

- [Authorization](/blog/documentation/laraue-boards/getting-started/authorization) — How to log in using your Telegram account, both inside the Mini App and in the browser. No password, no registration.

- [Quick start](/blog/documentation/laraue-boards/getting-started/quick-start) — A five-minute walkthrough: create a board, capture a message from Telegram, assign it, and move it through statuses.

## Before you begin

You need a Telegram account. That's it. There is no separate sign-up, no email to verify, and no credit card required.

Open [@laraue_boards_bot](https://t.me/laraue_boards_bot) in Telegram to get started, or visit [laraue.com/msgboard](https://laraue.com/msgboard) in your browser.
